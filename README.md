# SEO Party Skills

Experimental SEO skills for [OpenSEO](https://openseo.so), listed on [SEO.party](https://seo.party).

These aren't official OpenSEO skills. They're ideas for skills that we think people will find useful, usually for a narrower workflow than the core OpenSEO skills cover. Expect more of them over time.

Every skill here uses the OpenSEO MCP server, so you'll need OpenSEO connected to your agent.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [chatgpt-business-setup](skills/chatgpt-business-setup/SKILL.md) | Gets your business ready to claim on ChatGPT. Finds what customers search about your brand, checks what ChatGPT tells them today, and writes the profile to paste into Business Hub, the fixes to make on your site, the questions to test your Business Agent with, and the steps to claim and verify. Needs a paid OpenSEO plan. |
| [competitor-strategy](skills/competitor-strategy/SKILL.md) | Finds a site's competitors, then turns their top pages and backlinks into keywords to track, pages to create, and link themes. |
| [customer-keywords](skills/customer-keywords/SKILL.md) | Finds the searches your customer makes that you're missing, grouped into pages to build. Describe the customer and what they're looking for, or let it work that out from your site. |
| [free-tool](skills/free-tool/SKILL.md) | Finds the free tool your site should build for search traffic, checks that page one is winnable, and specs it so your agent can build it. |
| [keyword-gap](skills/keyword-gap/SKILL.md) | Finds keywords your competitors rank for and you don't, and groups them into page ideas. |
| [rank-on-ai](skills/rank-on-ai/SKILL.md) | Asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude what your buyers ask, shows where you rank in each answer, and lists the pages they cite that you should get onto. Needs a paid OpenSEO plan. |
| [reverse-engineer-seo](skills/reverse-engineer-seo/SKILL.md) | Reverse-engineers how any website gets its Google traffic: the page types that earn it, the search patterns behind them, and what attracts its links, then the moves to copy. |
| [seo-manager-agent](skills/seo-manager-agent/SKILL.md) | Sets up an SEO manager in OpenAI's dots, Grok Bot or Meta's Muse that checks your sites every week and sends what moved and what to do. Sets the schedule itself inside one of them, and walks you through it anywhere else. |

## Install

Find a skill on [SEO.party](https://seo.party) for install commands for your agent, or install with the GitHub CLI:

```sh
gh skill install every-app/seo-party-skills keyword-gap
```
